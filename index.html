About

I'm a product-minded software engineer at P&G, based in Warsaw, Poland. My career started in Mexico, working across operations and product at FREENOW, Mercado Libre, and Foxtrot Systems. I later joined P&G as a product manager, working on Pampers' global CRM and messaging ecosystem.

In 2025, I moved from defining products to building them, transitioning into mobile software engineering with Flutter and Dart. I also completed an MSc in Big Data & Advanced Analytics at SGH Warsaw School of Economics.

Today, I'm taking that journey further into agentic engineering. I build and orchestrate teams of AI agents, experiment with self-hosted infrastructure like Hermes, and develop products that turn AI into practical solutions.

I built FotoReady, helping small sellers in Mexico transform a single product photo into marketplace-ready images, and founded Adoptsi, focused on bringing AI agents and automation to traditional businesses.

What drives me: building AI systems that are reliable, testable, and affordable enough to solve real-world problems every day.
