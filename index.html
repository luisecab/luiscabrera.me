About

I'm a product-minded mobile software engineer at P&G, based in Warsaw, Poland. My career started in Mexico, working across operations and product at FREENOW, Mercado Libre, and Foxtrot Systems. I later joined P&G as a product manager, working on Pampers' global CRM and messaging ecosystem.

In 2026, I moved from defining products to building them, transitioning into mobile software engineering with Flutter and Dart. I also completed an MSc in Big Data & Advanced Analytics at SGH Warsaw School of Economics.

Today, I'm applying agentic engineering in the mobile space. In my current role I build mobile software with Flutter and Dart, working with teams of AI agents that I build and orchestrate. Outside of it, I experiment with self-hosted infrastructure like Hermes and develop products that turn AI into practical solutions.

I built FotoReady, helping small sellers in Mexico transform a single product photo into marketplace-ready images, and founded AdoptSI, focused on bringing AI agents and automation to traditional businesses.

What drives me: building AI systems that are reliable, testable, and affordable enough to solve real-world problems every day.
